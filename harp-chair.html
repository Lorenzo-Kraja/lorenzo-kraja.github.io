HARP CHAIR

2025 • PRODUCT & FURNITURE DESIGN
STATEMENT & MISSION
“My mission is to design a polypropylene monocoque chair that is stackable and perfect for outdoor use in historic venues. Inspired by 17th-century Windsor chairs, with their distinctive comb back and legs integrated into the seat, this chair combines historic elegance with the contemporary design of shell chairs. It also echoes the beveled, squared-off forms of the harp, offering a practical and stylistically refined solution.”
“The elegant curves and slender sticks of the Windsor design merge into a fascinating visual parallel, creating a chair that combines tradition and modernity with practicality and style.”
