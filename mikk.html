MIKK

2023 • SUSTAINABLE MOBILITY & AUTOMOTIVE DESIGN
STATEMENT & CONCEPT
“Current progress may not be sustainable for the planet, especially in the automotive sector, which focuses on large electric vehicles. My idea is to transform current habits into something more sustainable for the planet and humanity: smaller, lighter, and more efficient cars.”
“Mikk” is electric and is perfect for four people, designed for everyday use. It consumes little fuel and is lightweight, even with a small-to-medium battery pack. It also features front-wheel drive for improved agility in town.
Full-electric powertrain, front-wheel drive, lightweight chassis, 4-occupant compact architecture.
